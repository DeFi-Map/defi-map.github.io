Why it matters

Most dashboards tell you what you hold.
Few tell you how it got there.

Providing liquidity is not holding tokens. Its result depends on every deposit, withdrawal, fee collection and price move in between, and a balance number hides all of that.

01

Balance isn't performance

A position can be up 16% and still trail simply holding the same tokens. Without a holding benchmark you can't tell.

02

Fees can hide losses

Impermanent loss quietly eats fee income. If the two aren't separated, a losing position looks like a winner.

03

Costs add up

Minting, rebalancing, collecting and staking all cost gas. On small or busy positions it decides the outcome.

04

Positions are scattered

Several wallets, several protocols, staking wrappers on top. Comparing them by hand means spreadsheets.

What's different

Built as a ledger, not a snapshot

Most tools answer “how much do I have?”. DeFiMap is built to answer “did this position pay off, and why?”, which takes a different foundation.

Event-sourced

Replays history, not snapshots

Every mint, burn, fee collection and transfer is processed in order, so path-dependent numbers like impermanent loss are computed, not estimated from two balances.

MINT2.50 ETH · 5,000 USDC
COLLECT0.06 ETH · 118 USDC

NOW$10,740 principal · $914 fees
One ledger

Across protocols and wrappers

Uniswap V2, V3 and V4, Curve pools staked in Convex or StakeDAO, and Aave V3 sit in one PnL tree, so you can compare them on the same terms.

Own indexer

Computed from raw chain data

DeFiMap indexes on-chain events itself and does its own math, including concentrated-liquidity and stableswap formulas, instead of reselling aggregated numbers.

Honest coverage

Shows what it can't read yet

Positions in protocols that aren't indexed yet still appear with their value, flagged as not covered, instead of silently disappearing from your total.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to connect my wallet or sign anything?

No. You paste an address or import it from your browser wallet. DeFiMap only reads public on-chain data. It never asks for signatures, approvals or keys, and it can't move funds.

Which networks and protocols are supported?

Today: Ethereum mainnet with Uniswap V2, V3 and V4, Curve (including pools staked in Convex and StakeDAO) and Aave V3 positions. More chains and protocols are on the roadmap.

How is impermanent loss calculated?

Against simply holding the tokens you deposited. DeFiMap replays every deposit, withdrawal and transfer of the position in order, so adding or removing liquidity along the way is accounted for.

What if my protocol isn't supported yet?

Those positions still show up with their value, marked as not indexed, so your total stays honest. Requesting support for a new AMM is the next phase on the roadmap.

Can I use DeFiMap today?

DeFiMap is in closed beta. You sign in with Google, and access is currently by invitation. Open sign-up is the first phase on the roadmap.

Is this investment advice?

No. DeFiMap is an analytics tool. It shows what happened to your positions and why; decisions stay with you.
